About Me

I am a researcher in applied probability, stochastic control, Markov decision processes, and stochastic games. My work develops mathematically rigorous and computationally implementable decision models for systems affected by rare high-cost events, partial observation, and multiple time scales. I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati on 4 July 2023 under the supervision of Dr. Chandan Pal. My doctoral thesis was entitled Risk-sensitive Stochastic Control and Games.

Current position. I am a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at IIT Dharwad, India. I joined IIT Dharwad in September 2026 and currently teach Introduction to Mathematical Finance I (MA404T) and Stochastic Models (MA407T).

Computational methods

Value iteration, policy iteration, eigenvalue computation, Monte Carlo simulation, Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC), Gillespie simulation, and numerical implementation of stochastic processes; Python and C.

My current research programme includes risk-sensitive ergodic control of singularly perturbed continuous-time Markov decision processes, aggregated limit models, asymptotically optimal policies, partial-information control, mean-field games, and PDMDP/semi-Markov models.
